# Bank installment progress and history

Finances currently presents completed observed series before active ones and shows only N/T without explaining what remains. WhatsApp uses the same vague counter. Separate subscriptions, observed installments still below their total, series whose last installment was found, and unknown progress. Preserve every received row, bank identity, filter, value, unknown currency/date and transaction fact. No new provider requests, payment/deletion actions or financial totals are added.

Polp Celcoin [installments](https://polp.com.br/docs/celcoin/credit-cards/installments) defines paidInstallments as the highest observed charge_identificator, including when the history only includes 3/3. It is not proof that 3 payments occurred. [Bills](https://polp.com.br/docs/celcoin/credit-cards/bills) supplies cycle payment records which can include advance payments or a payment associated with the next cycle. Do not label a series paid/settled or allocate a partial bill payment to individual purchases without authoritative linkage.

For valid N of T, explain the last observed installment and T−N further installments expected according to received history. N=T goes in a separate last-installment section, with payment confirmation explicitly unknown. Missing/invalid counters stay unknown, never 0; individually valid numbers remain visible when the other counter is missing. Keep amounts per installment, do not infer BRL or next due date. Native disclosure for final/remaining lists avoids hiding active items behind the first four historical rows. The agenda appendix retains its 20-item bound. Structured and connected recurring query responses retain 10 items per bank, the received date, mode/company label, subtotal currency handling and incomplete warnings, while prioritizing ongoing information ahead of final series. Subtotals still represent one installment from every received series, including last observed installments, and never a remaining debt.
