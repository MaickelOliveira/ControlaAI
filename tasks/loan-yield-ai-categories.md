# Loan, yield and AI categories

Recognizable loan/consignado principal payments and receipts use Empréstimos. Loan interest, IOF and bank fees remain Encargos bancários. Recognizable received application yield, interest and dividends use Rendimentos; opaque provider INCOME_INTEREST_EARNED and INCOME_DIVIDENDS map to the same category.

ChatGPT/OpenAI, Anthropic/Claude subscriptions, Google Gemini and Perplexity charges use Inteligência Artificial. Generic Apple/Stripe descriptions do not identify a service. A person named Claude is not an AI subscription, and a course about ChatGPT remains Educação.

Manual category choices and exclusions win. Values, dates, currency, tenant/company scope and accounting inclusion do not change. An excluded redemption is not turned into income. Historic bank categories update when the ledger is read, with no bulk database writes. Existing manual categories are retained unless edited by their owner.

This is categorization only: it does not reconcile a credit-funded payment with its account counterpart or deduplicate financial amounts. That requires evidence linking the two source operations.
